Attack Fighter

AIR

Overview

Attack Fighters are specialised combat aircraft designed primarily to conduct attacks against ground and surface targets. They provide direct support to friendly ground forces while retaining the ability to operate in contested airspace.

Role

The primary role of the Attack Fighter is offensive operations against surface targets. Their high mobility and strong offensive capability allow them to rapidly respond to battlefield requirements and support ground operations.

Strengths

  • Strong offensive capability.
  • High mobility.
  • Effective against ground and surface targets.

Weaknesses

  • Limited defensive capability.
  • Cannot engage enemy air units and is limited to defensive air operations.
  • Vulnerable when operating without adequate air support.

Recommended Employment

Use Attack Fighters to support ground operations, strike enemy positions, and conduct offensive attacks against surface targets.
