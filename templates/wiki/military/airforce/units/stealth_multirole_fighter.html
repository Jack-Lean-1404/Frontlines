Stealth Multirole Fighter

AIR

Overview

Stealth Multirole Fighters are advanced combat aircraft designed to perform a wide range of missions while operating with a reduced presence on enemy detection systems. They combine multirole combat capabilities with advanced stealth technology, allowing them to operate effectively in contested airspace.

Role

The primary role of the Stealth Multirole Fighter is to provide a flexible combat aircraft capable of conducting both air-to-air and air-to-ground operations. Their stealth capabilities allow them to perform missions in areas where conventional aircraft may face increased detection and threat.

Special Capability

Stealth Multirole Fighters are exceptionally effective at Suppression of Enemy Air Defences (SEAD) operations. Their stealth capabilities allow them to approach and attack enemy air defence systems while reducing their exposure to hostile detection and interception.

Strengths

  • Highly versatile combat aircraft.
  • Advanced stealth capabilities.
  • Capable of performing multiple mission types.
  • Effective in contested airspace.

Weaknesses

  • Expensive to build and maintain.
  • High rare metal requirements.
  • Less specialised than dedicated air superiority aircraft.

Recommended Employment

Use Stealth Multirole Fighters to conduct high-risk operations in contested airspace, particularly Suppression of Enemy Air Defences (SEAD) missions. Their combination of stealth, mobility, and multirole capability makes them highly effective at targeting enemy air defence systems while remaining capable of engaging other targets when required.
