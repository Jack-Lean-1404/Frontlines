What is Frontlines?

Frontlines is a strategic modern warfare board game where players take command of a nation and compete for territory, resources, and strategic advantage across land, sea, and air.

Build your economy, develop cities and infrastructure, manage strategic resources, and establish the industrial capacity needed to support your nation. Raise and equip your military, expand your influence, and outmaneuver your opponents through careful planning and decisive action.

Every decision matters - from the resources you control to the forces you deploy.

THE WORLD OF FRONTLINES

Explore Alterra

Frontlines takes place on Alterra, an Earth-like world shaped by dramatic geological forces.

Around 60 million years ago, the ancient supercontinent of Primordia began to fracture and drift apart, eventually forming the five continents known today: Rivaran, Norland, Aurelia, Gaian, and Thalassia.

Each continent developed its own distinct geography and cultures, creating the diverse and evolving world in which Frontlines unfolds.
