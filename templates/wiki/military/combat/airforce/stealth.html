Stealth

Stealth aircraft are specialised aerial assets designed to reduce their detectability and increase their survivability during contested air operations. Stealth technology provides significant combat advantages, but stealth-capable aircraft are rare and strategically valuable.

Combat Effectiveness

Stealth aircraft are twice as effective as non-stealth aircraft in terms of both Strength and Defence.

This increased effectiveness represents the advanced sensors, materials, electronic systems, and combat capabilities associated with stealth aircraft.

Defence

Stealth aircraft have twice the Defence of equivalent non-stealth aircraft.

Anti-Air Evasion

Stealth aircraft are significantly more difficult for Anti-Air systems to detect and engage.

When a stealth aircraft is engaged by an Anti-Air unit, the attack has only a 20% chance of successfully hitting the aircraft.

By comparison, non-stealth aircraft have an 80% chance of being hit by Anti-Air fire.

The reduced hit chance reflects the difficulty of detecting and targeting stealth aircraft and their advanced countermeasures.

Strategic Role

Stealth aircraft are high-value assets intended for missions where reduced detectability and increased combat effectiveness provide a decisive advantage.

Their combination of superior combat effectiveness and resistance to Anti-Air fire makes them particularly valuable when operating in heavily defended airspace.
