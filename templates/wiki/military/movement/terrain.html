Terrain

Terrain represents the physical environment of each sector on the map and governs how units move and where they may operate. Each sector is assigned a Terrain Type, which determines its movement cost and any restrictions placed on military units.

Terrain Types

All terrain on the map is classified into one of five Terrain Types:

01

Open

Standard terrain with a movement cost of 1.

02

Rough

Difficult terrain with a movement cost of 2.

03

Severe

Difficult terrain with a movement cost of 2 and restrictions on several unit types.

04

Shallow Water

Shallow maritime terrain with a movement cost of 2.

05

Deep Water

Deep maritime terrain with a movement cost of 1 and restrictions on certain naval units.

Movement and Restrictions

The cost to move into a sector is determined by its Terrain Type. A unit must spend the required number of Movement Points to enter the sector.

A unit may not enter a sector if it is restricted by that sector's Terrain Type.

Terrain Movement Cost Restricted Units
Open 1 None
Rough 2 None
Severe 2 Mechanised Units, Armoured Units, Special Units
Shallow Water 2 Submarines
Deep Water 1 Carriers and Cruisers

Terrain Classification

River Crossing

Units may cross rivers when moving between sectors, but doing so increases the cost of movement.

Crossing a river requires 1 additional Movement Point.

If an Engineer unit is present in the same sector as the moving unit, this penalty may be ignored, representing the construction of a temporary bridge.

Terrain Names

Terrain names provide thematic flavour for the world of Alterra. The named terrain itself does not determine its gameplay behaviour.

Instead, every named terrain corresponds to a Terrain Type, and the Terrain Type determines its movement cost and unit restrictions.

For example, Mountain and Jungle are both classified as Severe terrain and therefore share the same movement cost and unit restrictions.

General Rule

Terrain affects movement and access only.

Terrain does not modify combat unless specified in another rule.
