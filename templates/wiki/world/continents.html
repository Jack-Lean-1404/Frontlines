Continents

The five continents of Alterra are the only major continental landmasses that remain above sea level. Separated by an enormous global ocean, each continent has developed its own geography, climate, societies, and political powers.

The Continents of Alterra

The modern continents were formed from the ancient supercontinent of Primordia following its fragmentation approximately 60 million years ago.

Over geological time, the five landmasses gradually separated and developed into the regions that exist today. Their isolation from one another has contributed to significant differences in geography, culture, and political development.

Continental Geography

Alterra's continents are separated by vast stretches of open ocean. Unlike Earth, there are no other large naturally occurring continental landmasses between them.

This geography has had a major influence on the development of civilisation. Coastal regions and major ports are particularly important, while long-distance travel between continents depends heavily on maritime transportation.

The geography of each continent also influences the strategic position of the nations located within it. Access to natural resources, coastlines, ports, major cities, and defensible terrain can all affect the balance of power.

A Connected World

Despite their separation, the continents are connected through extensive maritime trade and transportation networks.

The ocean serves as both a barrier and a highway between the continents. Nations capable of protecting their shipping routes and maintaining a strong naval presence can exert influence far beyond their own shores.
