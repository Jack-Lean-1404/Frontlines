Headquarters

LAND

Overview

Headquarters units provide command, control, communications, and coordination for military formations. They are responsible for planning operations, issuing orders, and ensuring that subordinate units operate effectively as part of a larger force.

Special Capabilities

Headquarters units command all units of the same or smaller size within a 2-sector radius.

Command & Control Learn how Headquarters coordinate friendly forces and how Disorganisation affects units.

Strengths

  • Enables coordinated operations.
  • Supports multiple formations simultaneously.
  • Essential for large-scale military campaigns.

Weaknesses

  • Limited combat capability.
  • High-value target.
  • Loss of an HQ can severely disrupt nearby forces.

Recommended Employment

Use Headquarters near the front line to command nearby units and coordinate large operations.
