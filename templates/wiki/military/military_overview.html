Military

The military system in Frontlines represents the armed forces of Alterra's nations and alliances across land, sea, and air. Players command military forces, manoeuvre units across the map, conduct combat operations, and maintain the logistics and command networks required to sustain their forces.

Military Forces

Military forces are divided into four primary categories: Army, Navy, Air Force, and Special Units. Each category contains different units designed to perform specific roles during military operations.

Successful military operations require players to combine different types of forces. Combat units provide offensive and defensive capabilities, while specialised and support units provide additional capabilities required to conduct sustained operations.

Combat

Combat occurs when opposing forces engage during military operations. Different forms of combat use different rules depending on the units and domains involved.

Land, air, naval, and specialised combat each have their own mechanics and considerations.

Combat Explore the combat systems used across land, sea, and air operations.

Movement

Military units move between sectors during the Action Phase. Movement is affected by unit capabilities, available Movement Points, terrain, and other movement restrictions.

Units must complete their movement before initiating combat, and movement cannot be resumed after combat has been initiated.

Movement Learn how units move between sectors and how movement restrictions affect military operations. Terrain Learn how terrain types affect movement and unit access.

Logistics

Military forces require logistical support to maintain sustained operations. Supply allows units to continue operating effectively across the battlefield.

Logistics infrastructure and dedicated logistics units allow nations to maintain forces away from major population centres and production areas.

Logistics Learn how military forces are supplied and how logistics support operations.

Command & Control

Command and control allows military forces to operate as coordinated formations. Headquarters provide command capabilities to friendly forces within their effective range.

Units operating outside appropriate command support can become Disorganised and suffer reduced effectiveness.

Command & Control Learn how Headquarters coordinate friendly forces and how Disorganisation affects units.

Military Operations

Military operations combine movement, combat, logistics, command, and specialised capabilities to achieve strategic objectives.

Successful operations require more than simply fielding powerful units. Players must consider the terrain, supply network, command structure, available reinforcements, and capabilities of both friendly and enemy forces.
