Frigate

SEA

Overview

Frigates are versatile surface combatants designed to provide affordable naval firepower and support for larger naval formations. They offer a balance of combat capability, mobility, and cost, making them useful for a wide range of surface operations.

Role

The primary role of the Frigate is to provide general-purpose surface combat capability. Frigates can engage enemy surface vessels and support larger naval formations, but lack the specialised capabilities of Destroyers.

Strengths

  • Lower cost than Destroyers.
  • Good balance of mobility and combat power.
  • Effective against enemy surface vessels.
  • Useful for supporting larger naval formations.

Weaknesses

  • Cannot conduct Anti-Submarine Warfare.
  • Less combat power than Destroyers.
  • Less capable of operating independently against specialised naval threats.

Recommended Employment

Use Frigates as affordable surface combatants to supplement larger naval formations, engage enemy surface vessels, and maintain a naval presence across multiple areas. Their lower cost makes them useful when a larger number of surface combatants is required.
