Aircraft Carrier

SEA

Overview

Aircraft Carriers are large naval vessels that function as mobile air bases, allowing nations to project air power far beyond the range of land-based airports. Their ability to operate aircraft at sea makes them the centrepiece of carrier-based naval operations.

Role

The primary role of the Aircraft Carrier is to provide an operational base for embarked Naval Multi-Role Fighters. Carriers allow NMRFs to conduct air operations from sea while also providing Anti-Air defence for the carrier group.

Strengths

  • Provides a mobile airbase at sea.
  • Allows NMRFs to operate far from land-based airports.
  • Provides Anti-Air defence against hostile aircraft.
  • Enables sustained air operations across large areas.

Weaknesses

  • Extremely expensive to construct.
  • Very high resource and upkeep requirements.
  • High-value target for enemy forces.
  • Requires protection from enemy naval, submarine, and air threats.
  • Limited direct combat capability compared with dedicated surface combatants.

Carrier Operations

Aircraft Carriers provide mobile operational bases for Naval Multi-Role Fighters. Embarked NMRFs can use the carrier as an operational anchor in the same way aircraft use a friendly airport.

This allows carrier-based aircraft to conduct operations far from friendly territory and project air power across contested areas.

Carrier Operations Learn how Aircraft Carriers operate aircraft and project air power at sea.

Anti-Air Defence

Aircraft Carriers provide Anti-Air defence against hostile aircraft operating against the carrier and its surrounding naval forces.

This defensive capability helps protect the carrier and its embarked aircraft from enemy air operations.

Recommended Employment

Use Aircraft Carriers as the centrepiece of carrier battle groups and major naval operations. They are particularly valuable when air power needs to be projected far from friendly airports.

Due to their enormous cost and strategic importance, Carriers should operate alongside surface combatants and other supporting naval forces rather than operating independently.
