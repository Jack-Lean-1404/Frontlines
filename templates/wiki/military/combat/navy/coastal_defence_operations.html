Coastal Defence

Coastal Defence provides long-range protection for coastlines against hostile naval forces. Coastal Defence units create defensive zones that automatically engage enemy naval units operating near defended territory.

Coastal Defence Learn about Coastal Defence capabilities, statistics, and unit-specific rules.

Coastal Defence Zone

Each Coastal Defence unit establishes a Coastal Defence Zone extending 2 sectors around its position.

The Coastal Defence Zone remains at 2 sectors regardless of the formation level of the Coastal Defence unit.

Automatic Engagement

Enemy naval units that interact with a Coastal Defence Zone are automatically engaged by the Coastal Defence unit.

Any interaction with the zone is sufficient to trigger an engagement. Coastal Defence therefore acts as a form of long-range coastal area denial.

Coastal Defence Attack

Coastal Defence does not use the standard Strength vs Defence combat system.

Instead, each engagement uses a fixed 60% chance of successfully hitting the targeted naval unit.

Sector Radius

Coastal Defence protects an area extending 2 sectors from its position.

Hit Chance

Each Coastal Defence engagement has a 60% chance of successfully hitting its target.

Successful Engagement

If the Coastal Defence attack is successful, the targeted naval formation suffers one formation size loss.

If the naval formation is already at its smallest available formation level, another formation size loss destroys the formation.

Failed Engagement

If the 60% attack chance fails, the engagement has no effect on the targeted naval unit.

Layered Coastal Defence

Multiple Coastal Defence units can protect the same area, allowing players to establish layered coastal defences.

Each applicable Coastal Defence unit engages the naval unit independently.

This allows multiple Coastal Defence units to threaten naval forces operating within overlapping Coastal Defence Zones.

Formation Level

The formation level of a Coastal Defence unit does not change the size of its Coastal Defence Zone or its 60% hit chance.

Formation size only represents the remaining effective strength of the Coastal Defence formation.

Strategic Role

Coastal Defence provides long-range coastal area denial, making naval operations near defended coastlines more dangerous.

Its fixed hit chance makes Coastal Defence less reliable than standard Air Defence against non-stealth aircraft, but its extended range allows it to threaten naval forces across a larger area.
