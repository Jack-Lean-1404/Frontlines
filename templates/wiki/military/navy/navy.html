Navy

The Navy provides the primary surface maritime forces of the nations of Alterra. Naval forces are responsible for controlling sea areas, protecting maritime routes, supporting coastal operations, and projecting military power across the world's oceans.

Because Alterra is dominated by ocean, naval power plays an important role in military strategy. Control of important sea routes, coastal areas, ports, and maritime approaches can directly influence the outcome of military campaigns.

Naval Forces

The Navy is primarily composed of surface vessels and specialised maritime support vessels. These forces provide the conventional fleet capabilities required to control maritime areas and conduct naval operations.

Surface Fleet

Surface combatants form the backbone of conventional naval forces. These vessels provide offensive firepower, fleet protection, and the ability to project military power across large areas of ocean.

Surface vessels range from smaller combatants such as Corvettes and Frigates to larger vessels such as Destroyers and Cruisers. Each class provides different capabilities and operational roles.

Surface Fleet Explore surface combatants and their roles in maritime warfare.

Naval Operations

Naval forces can perform a variety of specialised operations beyond direct fleet combat.

These operations can include transporting forces and supplies, supporting amphibious operations, protecting maritime routes, and maintaining naval forces away from their home ports.

Naval Operations Explore specialised maritime operations and naval support capabilities.

Maritime Warfare

Naval warfare is primarily concerned with controlling maritime areas and defeating opposing surface forces.

The positioning of naval forces is particularly important because the vast oceans of Alterra create large operational areas between major landmasses.

Naval forces can be used to protect friendly shipping, threaten enemy maritime routes, support coastal operations, and project military power far from national territory.

Naval Combat Learn how engagements between opposing naval forces are resolved.

Amphibious Operations

Naval forces can support operations that move military forces between maritime and land environments.

These operations require coordination between naval forces and ground formations and can provide commanders with additional options for attacking or reinforcing coastal areas.

Naval Operations Explore amphibious operations and other specialised maritime missions.

Naval Logistics

Naval forces require appropriate infrastructure and logistical support to sustain prolonged operations.

Ports and specialised naval support vessels can allow fleets to operate farther from their home territory and maintain their operational capabilities during extended campaigns.

Logistics Learn how military forces are supplied and sustained during operations.

Special Naval Capabilities

Not all naval warfare capabilities are part of the conventional Navy. Some specialised maritime forces are classified as Special Units and operate under their own rules.

Submarines are classified as Special Units rather than conventional Navy forces. Their ability to operate beneath the ocean surface provides a specialised capability distinct from the surface fleet.

Coastal Defence units are also classified as Special Units. These forces provide specialised protection for coastal areas and maritime approaches.

Special Units Explore specialised military capabilities including Submarines and Coastal Defence.

Strategic Importance of Naval Power

The geography of Alterra makes naval power strategically important to almost every major nation.

Oceans separate the world's continents and create important maritime routes connecting nations, ports, and economic centres. A nation capable of controlling these routes can influence both military operations and international trade.

Naval forces can therefore serve both offensive and defensive purposes, from protecting national coastlines to projecting military power across distant oceans.
