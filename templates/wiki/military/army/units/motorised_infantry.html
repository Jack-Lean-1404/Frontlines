Motorised Infantry

LAND

Overview

Motorised Infantry are the backbone of any nation's military. They provide a balance between the mobility of mechanised forces and the flexibility of traditional infantry, making them a versatile element on the modern battlefield.

Their ability to rapidly redeploy across a wide range of terrain allows them to respond quickly to changing battlefield conditions.

Special Capabilities

Motorised Infantry can cross all terrain with only a -1 movement penalty.

Terrain Learn how terrain affects movement costs and unit access.

Strengths

  • Low cost.
  • Highly versatile.
  • Effective across all terrain types.

Weaknesses

  • Limited protection compared to mechanised or armoured forces.
  • Vulnerable in prolonged engagements against heavier units.

Recommended Employment

Use Motorised Infantry to capture territory, reinforce the front line, and operate in difficult terrain.
