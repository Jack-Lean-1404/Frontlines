Armoured

LAND

Overview

Armoured units provide the firepower, mobility, and protection required for breakthrough operations and battlefield dominance. They are often tasked with spearheading offensives, exploiting weaknesses in enemy lines, and supporting infantry formations during major operations.

Special Capabilities

When in the same sector as a Mechanised Infantry unit, Armoured gains a +1 bonus to the Strength roll per unit level during combat.

Mechanised Infantry Learn more about Mechanised Infantry and their role in combined-arms operations. Land Combat Learn how engagements between opposing ground forces are resolved.

Strengths

  • Highest combat power among ground units.
  • Excellent protection and survivability.
  • Highly effective in offensive operations.

Weaknesses

  • Expensive to build and maintain.
  • High fuel consumption.
  • Dependent on support from infantry and logistics units.

Recommended Employment

Use Armoured units to lead major offensives and destroy enemy frontline units.
