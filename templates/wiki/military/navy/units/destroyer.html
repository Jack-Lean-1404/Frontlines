Destroyer

SEA

Overview

Destroyers are powerful and versatile surface combatants designed to protect naval formations and engage a wide range of enemy threats. They provide greater combat capability than Frigates while also providing specialised Anti-Submarine Warfare capabilities.

Role

The primary role of the Destroyer is to provide strong surface combat capability while protecting friendly naval forces from submarine threats. Destroyers are well suited to operating alongside larger naval formations and engaging enemy surface forces.

Strengths

  • Capable of conducting Anti-Submarine Warfare.
  • Effective when operating alongside larger naval formations.
  • Good balance of firepower, protection, and mobility.

Weaknesses

  • More expensive than Frigates.
  • Higher resource and upkeep requirements.
  • Requires logistical support during sustained operations.

Special Capability

Destroyers are specialised Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) vessels. They can detect and engage enemy submarines, providing protection for friendly naval formations against underwater threats.

Submarine Warfare Learn how Destroyers detect and engage enemy submarines.

Recommended Employment

Use Destroyers as frontline surface combatants and escorts for larger naval formations. Their combination of strong combat capability and Anti-Submarine Warfare makes them particularly valuable for protecting fleets from submarine threats while engaging enemy surface forces.
