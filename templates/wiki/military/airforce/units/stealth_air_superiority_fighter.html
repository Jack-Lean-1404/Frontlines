Stealth Air Superiority Fighter

AIR

Overview

Stealth Air Superiority Fighters are advanced combat aircraft designed to establish and maintain control of the airspace while operating with a reduced presence on enemy detection systems. They combine strong air-to-air combat capabilities with advanced stealth technology.

Role

The primary role of the Stealth Air Superiority Fighter is to engage and defeat enemy aircraft while operating in contested airspace. Their combination of high combat capability and stealth makes them particularly suited to air superiority and interception missions.

Strengths

  • Excellent air-to-air combat capability.
  • Advanced stealth capabilities.
  • Strong defensive capability.
  • Highly effective for establishing air superiority.

Weaknesses

  • Extremely expensive to build and maintain.
  • High rare metal requirements.
  • More specialised than Multirole Fighters.

Recommended Employment

Use Stealth Air Superiority Fighters to establish air superiority, intercept enemy aircraft, and conduct operations in contested airspace where stealth provides an advantage.
