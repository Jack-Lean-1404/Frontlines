Resupply Ship

SEA

Overview

Resupply Ships are dedicated naval logistics vessels that provide fuel, ammunition, equipment, and other supplies to friendly naval forces operating at sea. They allow fleets to remain operational away from friendly ports and established supply infrastructure.

Role

The primary role of the Resupply Ship is to provide logistical support to naval formations. They act as mobile supply platforms, extending the operational endurance of friendly naval forces and supporting sustained operations at sea.

Strengths

  • Provides logistical support at sea.
  • Extends the operational range of naval formations.
  • Allows fleets to operate away from friendly ports for longer periods.

Weaknesses

  • Very limited combat capability.
  • High-value logistical target.
  • Requires protection from frontline combatants.

Special Capability

Resupply Ships function as Logistics units at sea, providing supplies to friendly naval units operating within their supply range.

A Resupply Ship can provide supplies to friendly naval units within a 2-sector radius, provided the Resupply Ship is the same size or larger than the naval unit receiving supply.

Recommended Employment

Use Resupply Ships to support fleets operating away from friendly ports and naval bases. Position them within 2 sectors of the naval forces they are supporting while ensuring the Resupply Ship is large enough to supply the intended formations.

Protecting Resupply Ships is important because losing naval logistics can significantly reduce the ability of a fleet to sustain operations.
