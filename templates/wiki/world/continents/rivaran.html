Rivaran

Rivaran is the easternmost continent of Alterra. Home to diverse environments ranging from northern tundra and central forests to western rainforests and eastern deserts, Rivaran is one of the world's most geographically varied regions.

Rivaran, showing national territories and major biomes.

Geography

Rivaran is the easternmost of Alterra's five continents. It is bordered by Norland to the northwest, Gaian to the west, and Thalassia to the south. The Horizon Ocean lies to the east.

The continent is characterised by a highly varied landscape. Mountains, forests, plains, savannas, deserts, tundra, and rainforest all occur within Rivaran.

At the centre of the continent lies the Zenith Mountains, a major mountain system that strongly influences Rivaran's climate and river systems.

The Zenith Mountains

The Zenith Mountains form the geographic centre of Rivaran and represent one of the continent's most important natural features.

The mountains contain extensive areas of snow and ice. Meltwater from the Zenith Mountains, combined with rainfall, feeds several of Rivaran's major river systems.

The mountains also have a major influence on the continent's climate. Moisture carried across the continent is forced upward by the mountains, producing significant rainfall on the wetter sides. Areas beyond the mountains receive considerably less precipitation, contributing to the dry environments found across eastern Rivaran.

Major Rivers

Tiamy River

The Tiamy River originates in the Zenith Mountains and flows northeast toward the ocean.

Fed primarily by melting snow and ice from the mountains along with rainfall, the river provides a major source of freshwater for the Commonwealth of Tiamat.

The surrounding plains form one of Rivaran's most productive agricultural regions. The fertile Tiamy basin is a major contributor to Tiamat's food production and has earned the country a reputation as one of the continent's principal breadbaskets.

Verde River

The Verde River flows northward from the Zenith Mountains toward Rivaran's northern coast.

The river forms a major natural boundary between the Commonwealth of Tiamat and the Federal Socialist Republic of Azerland.

Like the Tiamy, the Verde is sustained by precipitation and mountain meltwater from the Zenith region, making it an important source of freshwater for the surrounding regions.

Revolution River

The Revolution River lies west of the Verde River within the territory of the Federal Socialist Republic of Azerland.

Rather than forming an international border, the river divides two regions of Azerland and is an important geographical feature within the country.

Climate & Biomes

Rivaran contains some of the greatest environmental variation found anywhere on Alterra. The central Zenith Mountains divide major climatic regions and contribute to the continent's distinctive rainfall patterns.

NORTH

Tundra

The northern regions of Rivaran are dominated by cold tundra environments. Low temperatures and limited vegetation make the region one of the continent's harshest environments.

WEST

Rainforest

Dense rainforest extends across much of western Rivaran, stretching toward the Tempest Sea. High rainfall and warm conditions support an extensive jungle ecosystem.

CENTRAL

Forests

Large forest regions occupy the areas surrounding the continent's major river systems, particularly between the Verde and Tiamy rivers.

CENTRAL & EAST

Plains

Extensive plains support agriculture and settlement across central and eastern Rivaran. The fertile plains around the Tiamy are particularly important to Tiamat.

SOUTH

Savanna

South of the Zenith Mountains lies a broad savanna region extending toward Thalassia.

EAST

Desert

A large desert occupies much of eastern Rivaran. The region lies within the rain shadow of the Zenith Mountains and receives significantly less rainfall than western areas.

Bellavistian Archipelago

Off the southeastern coast of Rivaran lies the island chain controlled by the Bellavistian Theocratic Republic.

The archipelago is heavily forested and forms a distinctive geographic region separated from mainland Rivaran by open water. Bellavistia's position gives it control over a significant portion of the southeastern maritime approaches.

The Western Seas

Tempest Sea

The Tempest Sea lies along Rivaran's western coast and separates much of the continent from the neighbouring landmass of Gaian.

Aurum Strait

The Aurum Strait lies southwest of Rivaran and provides a major maritime passage between the continent and the surrounding waters.

The Great Ocean Routes

Rivaran occupies a strategically important position along Alterra's global maritime network.

The extreme depths, severe weather, powerful currents, and other hazards found throughout the open ocean make long-distance navigation across Alterra extremely dangerous.

As a result, the Great Northern Route and Great Southern Route provide the only reliably navigable corridors for ships seeking to travel around the planet.

Great Northern Route

The Great Northern Route runs east-west across the northern ocean. It provides one of the two principal safe passages for long-distance maritime travel around Alterra.

Great Southern Route

The Great Southern Route runs east-west across the southern ocean and provides the second major safe passage around the planet.

Together, the two routes form critical links between the continents and are among the most important maritime trade corridors in the world.

Strategic Importance

Rivaran's geography gives it considerable strategic importance. Its position between several continents and the Horizon Ocean places it at the centre of major maritime and commercial routes.

Control of the Great Northern and Great Southern Routes can influence global trade, while control of major ports and coastal territory provides nations with access to these vital corridors.

The continent's rivers and fertile agricultural regions are equally important. Freshwater from the Zenith Mountains supports large populations and productive farmland, while the continent's varied terrain creates natural defensive positions and distinct strategic regions.
