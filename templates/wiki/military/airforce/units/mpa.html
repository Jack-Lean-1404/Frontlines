Marine Patrol Aircraft

AIR

Overview

Marine Patrol Aircraft (MPA) are specialised aircraft designed to conduct maritime patrol and Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) operations. They provide an aerial capability for detecting and engaging enemy submarines while supporting friendly naval forces.

Role

The primary role of the Marine Patrol Aircraft is to conduct Anti-Submarine Warfare operations. MPA can detect and attack enemy submarines, providing an important counter to submarine threats operating against friendly naval forces.

Strengths

  • Specialised Anti-Submarine Warfare capability.
  • Can detect and attack enemy submarines.
  • Cannot be attacked by submarines in return.
  • Can operate independently of surface naval forces.

Weaknesses

  • Limited defensive capability.
  • Lower combat capability than dedicated combat aircraft.
  • Primarily specialised for maritime and Anti-Submarine Warfare operations.
  • Vulnerable to enemy aircraft.

Special Capability

Marine Patrol Aircraft are specialised Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) aircraft. They can detect and attack enemy submarines.

MPA cannot be attacked by submarines in return because they are aircraft rather than naval units. Submarines therefore cannot use their specialised submarine attack against MPA.

Recommended Employment

Use Marine Patrol Aircraft to locate and attack enemy submarines and protect friendly naval forces from underwater threats. MPA are particularly valuable when operating alongside surface fleets where they can provide dedicated Anti-Submarine Warfare coverage.
