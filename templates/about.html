ABOUT FRONTLINES

The Story Behind Frontlines

From a simple board game made for friends to an ambitious strategy game built from the ground up.

WHAT IS FRONTLINES?

A Game Built From An Idea

Frontlines is an independently developed strategy game created by Jack Lean.

It combines game design, programming, worldbuilding, economics, military systems and web development into one project.

At its heart, Frontlines exists to create the kind of strategy game that I have always wanted to play — while creating something that friends can enjoy together.

FRONTLINES
THE BEGINNING
WHERE IT STARTED

It Started At School

I've always loved 4X and strategy games, but I often felt that there was something missing.

I wanted a strategy game focused on modern warfare, modern technology and modern military units. Instead, many of the games I found were focused on science fiction, fantasy, or historical periods.

So I decided to make my own.

The first version was a simple board game inspired by games like Risk, made for my friends to play at school.

THE EVOLUTION

Years Of Iteration

The game didn't become Frontlines overnight.

Over time, the project went through many different versions and countless games with friends.

Projects such as Rising Storm, Rising Storm Remastered, and Geopolitics each introduced new ideas, mechanics and lessons.

Eventually, those ideas came together to form Frontlines.
