Frontlines Overview

Frontlines is a strategic modern warfare game where players command nations, develop their economies, build military forces, and compete for strategic advantage across the world of Alterra.

What is Frontlines?

Frontlines places players in command of a nation within a dynamic world of competing interests, economic development, diplomacy, and military conflict.

Players must manage the resources and infrastructure of their nation while developing the industrial capacity required to support their ambitions. At the same time, nations must maintain military forces, establish diplomatic relationships, and respond to threats from other players.

Building Your Nation

A nation's strength is built on its economic and industrial foundation. Players can develop cities, construct infrastructure, manage resources, establish firms, and expand their production capabilities.

Economic decisions directly influence a nation's ability to produce military equipment, maintain its forces, and sustain long-term development.

Commanding the Military

Military forces allow nations to defend their territory and project power beyond their borders.

Frontlines features land, naval, and air forces, each serving different strategic roles. Successful commanders must consider the composition of their forces, logistics, production capacity, and the wider strategic situation when planning military operations.

The World of Alterra

Frontlines takes place on Alterra, an Earth-like world consisting of five major continents: Rivaran, Norland, Aurelia, Gaian, and Thalassia.

These continents contain the nations, cities, resources, and strategic locations that form the game world.
