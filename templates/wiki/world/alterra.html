Alterra

Alterra is a water-dominated world where the vast majority of the planet's surface is covered by an exceptionally deep ocean. Above sea level, only five major continental landmasses remain: Rivaran, Norland, Aurelia, Gaian, and Thalassia.

The five major landmasses of Alterra.

A World of Ocean

Unlike Earth, Alterra is dominated by water. The planet's oceans cover almost its entire surface, with only a small fraction of the world's land remaining above sea level.

The five continental landmasses are the only major naturally occurring areas of land above sea level. Beyond them, the world extends into an enormous and largely unexplored ocean.

The extreme depth of Alterra's oceans has made large portions of the planet difficult to explore. While the coastal waters surrounding the continents are well known, much of the deep ocean remains remote and poorly understood.

The Formation of Primordia

The origins of Alterra's modern geography can be traced back approximately 60 million years to the formation of Primordia.

During this period, enormous geological forces reshaped the planet. A vast supervolcanic event created a massive area of elevated continental crust that rose above the surrounding ocean.

This immense landmass became known as Primordia.

Over millions of years, geological activity caused Primordia to fracture and separate. The resulting landmasses gradually moved apart, eventually forming the five continents that exist today.

The Five Continents

The five continents are the only major landmasses on Alterra that remain permanently above sea level. Each developed its own geography, ecosystems, cultures, and political powers over millions of years.

The Deep Ocean

Beyond the continental landmasses lies an immense ocean extending across almost the entire planet. In many regions, the seafloor lies far below the surface, creating vast areas of deep and open water.

The deepest regions of Alterra remain among the least explored environments on the planet. Extreme depths, distance from land, and the difficulty of operating in the open ocean have limited exploration of these areas.

The ocean is not simply a barrier between the continents. It is a fundamental part of Alterra's geography and plays an important role in transportation, trade, resource extraction, fishing, and warfare.

Life on Alterra

The dominance of the ocean has had a profound influence on the development of life across the planet.

Coastal environments support large populations and many of the world's major cities, while marine ecosystems occupy the vast majority of the planet's habitable environment.

The full diversity of life found within Alterra's deepest waters remains poorly understood.

Human Civilisation

Almost all major human civilisation is concentrated on the five continental landmasses and their surrounding coastal regions.

This concentration has made access to land, ports, natural resources, and maritime routes strategically important to nations across Alterra.

The ocean also acts as both a connection and a barrier between societies. Nations separated by thousands of kilometres of open water must rely on maritime infrastructure and naval forces to maintain trade, communication, and military operations.

The Strategic Importance of the Ocean

Because the majority of Alterra is ocean, control of the seas is an important component of national power.

Naval forces allow nations to protect shipping, defend coastlines, project military power, and maintain access to distant territories. Ports and maritime infrastructure can therefore become critical strategic assets.

Offshore resources also contribute to national economies. Oil extraction, fishing, maritime transportation, and other ocean-based industries connect the economies of the continents to the wider world.
