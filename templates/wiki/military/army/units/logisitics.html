Logistics

LAND

Overview

Logistics units ensure that military formations remain supplied with fuel, ammunition, equipment, and other critical resources. Without logistics support, military operations quickly lose effectiveness. These units form the backbone of any sustained campaign.

Special Capabilities

Logistics units supply all units of the same or smaller size within a 2-sector radius.

Logistics Learn how military forces are supplied and sustained during operations.

Strengths

  • Extends operational reach.
  • Supports large formations.
  • Essential for sustained military operations.

Weaknesses

  • Limited combat capability.
  • High-value target for enemy forces.
  • Loss of logistics support can disrupt entire operations.

Recommended Employment

Use Logistics units behind the front line to keep nearby units supplied and combat effective.
