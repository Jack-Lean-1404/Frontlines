Sealift

SEA

Overview

Sealift units are specialised transport vessels designed to move ground forces across the sea. They provide a reliable method of transporting military units between friendly coastal sectors and supporting large-scale ground operations.

Role

The primary role of Sealift is to transport ground units across the sea. Sealift Ships allow ground forces to be repositioned between coastal areas without requiring amphibious assault operations.

Strengths

  • Provides strategic ground-force transport across the sea.
  • High movement capability.
  • Can transport large ground formations when operating at higher sizes.
  • Useful for repositioning forces between distant coastal sectors.

Weaknesses

  • Can only transport one ground unit at a time.
  • Vulnerable to enemy naval and air attacks.
  • Requires protection when operating in contested waters.

Transport Capacity

A Sealift Ship may carry one ground unit that is the same size or smaller than the Sealift Ship.

Only one ground unit may be carried by a Sealift Ship at any given time.

Transport capacity is determined by the size of the Sealift Ship.

01

Size 1

Can carry one Size 1 ground unit.

02

Size 2

Can carry one Size 2, Size 1, or smaller ground unit.

03

Size 3

Can carry one Size 3, Size 2, or Size 1 ground unit.

Recommended Employment

Use Sealift units to move ground forces between coastal sectors and establish or reinforce positions across the sea. Sealift Ships should generally operate alongside naval forces when travelling through contested waters.

Their limited combat capability makes protecting Sealift Ships important, particularly when transporting valuable ground formations.
