Strategic Airlift Aircraft

AIR

Overview

Strategic Airlift Aircraft (SAA) are specialised transport aircraft designed to rapidly deploy ground forces across long distances. They provide commanders with the ability to reposition eligible formations between friendly airbases or deploy forces directly into operational areas through airdropping.

Role

The primary role of the Strategic Airlift Aircraft is the strategic movement and deployment of ground forces. SAAs can transport eligible ground units and Attack Helicopters up to their own formation size.

Armoured units cannot be transported by Strategic Airlift Aircraft.

Strengths

  • Rapidly deploys ground forces across long distances.
  • Can transport eligible formations between friendly airbases.
  • Can airdrop eligible forces directly into operational areas.
  • Can use Tankers as operational anchor points.

Weaknesses

  • Cannot transport Armoured units.
  • Cannot conduct offensive combat operations.
  • Limited defensive capability.
  • Vulnerable to enemy Air Defence during airdrop operations.

Special Capability

Strategic Airlift Aircraft can conduct both Standard Airlift and Airdrop operations.

Standard Airlift allows eligible units to be transported safely between friendly airbases. Airdrop allows eligible units to be deployed directly into friendly, contested, or enemy-controlled sectors.

Recommended Employment

Use Strategic Airlift Aircraft to rapidly reposition ground forces, reinforce distant fronts, and deploy eligible formations into operational areas. Use Standard Airlift for safe strategic redeployment and Airdrop when forces need to be inserted where conventional deployment is not possible.
