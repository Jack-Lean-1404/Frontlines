Stealth Bomber

AIR

Overview

Stealth Bombers are advanced long-range aircraft designed to penetrate heavily defended airspace and deliver powerful attacks against enemy ground targets and buildings. Their stealth capabilities provide increased survivability when operating against defended targets.

Role

The primary role of the Stealth Bomber is to conduct strategic bombing operations against high-value enemy ground targets and buildings. Stealth Bombers are specialised for deep-strike missions and cannot engage enemy air or naval units.

Combat Restrictions

Stealth Bombers can only attack ground units and buildings. They cannot attack enemy air or naval units.

Their advanced stealth capabilities make them particularly suited to striking defended targets deep within enemy territory.

Strengths

  • Extremely high offensive capability.
  • High defensive capability compared with conventional Bombers.
  • Very long operational range.
  • Highly effective against important ground targets and buildings.

Weaknesses

  • Extremely expensive to build and maintain.
  • High resource and fuel requirements.
  • Cannot attack enemy air or naval units.
  • Requires careful employment against heavily defended targets.

Recommended Employment

Use Stealth Bombers to conduct deep-strike and strategic bombing operations against high-value enemy buildings and ground targets. Their advanced capabilities make them particularly valuable for attacking important targets in heavily defended areas.
