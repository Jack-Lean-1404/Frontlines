Tanker

AIR

Overview

Tanker aircraft provide aerial refuelling capabilities that allow military aircraft to extend their operational range and remain in the air for longer periods. They are essential support aircraft for sustained and long-range air operations.

Role

The primary role of the Tanker is to support friendly aircraft by providing aerial refuelling. Tankers allow combat aircraft to operate farther from their bases and remain operational for extended periods.

Strengths

  • Extends the operational range of friendly aircraft.
  • Supports sustained air operations.
  • Enables long-range air missions.

Weaknesses

  • Cannot conduct offensive combat operations.
  • Limited defensive capability.
  • High-value support aircraft.
  • Requires protection from enemy combat aircraft.

Special Capability

Tankers function as mobile operational anchor points for friendly aircraft. They allow aircraft to operate beyond the range of fixed airbases while remaining within their operational limits.

Tankers as Anchor Points

Aerial refuelling aircraft (Tankers) function as mobile anchor points for friendly aircraft. Aircraft may operate up to their movement range from a Tanker in the same way they operate from an Airport.

Tankers allow aircraft to remain within operational limits while conducting missions far from fixed airbases.

Recommended Employment

Use Tankers to extend the operational reach of friendly aircraft and support missions conducted far from fixed airbases. Position Tankers carefully to allow combat aircraft to operate within their operational limits while maintaining access to their required areas of operation.
