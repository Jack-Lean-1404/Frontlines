Cruiser

SEA

Overview

Cruisers are powerful surface combatants designed to destroy enemy naval forces and establish superiority at sea. Their heavy armament and substantial combat capability make them the most effective conventional surface combatants available.

Role

The primary role of the Cruiser is to destroy enemy naval formations. Cruisers are intended to serve as major offensive assets within a fleet, using their high combat power to overwhelm enemy surface forces.

Strengths

  • Highly effective against enemy naval forces.
  • Strong offensive capability.
  • Large formation size provides substantial combat capacity.
  • Excellent choice for major fleet engagements.

Weaknesses

  • Very expensive to construct.
  • High resource and upkeep requirements.
  • Losses can be costly due to the value of each formation.
  • Less efficient for routine patrol and escort duties than smaller surface combatants.

Recommended Employment

Use Cruisers when the primary objective is to destroy enemy naval forces. They are particularly effective in major fleet engagements where their high combat power can be used to overwhelm enemy surface formations.

Due to their high cost, Cruisers should be deployed deliberately and supported by other naval units rather than exposed unnecessarily to enemy attacks.
