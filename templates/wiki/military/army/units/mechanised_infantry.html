Mechanised Infantry

LAND

Overview

Mechanised Infantry units are highly mobile and versatile forces transported and protected by armoured vehicles. They are capable of engaging in a wide variety of combat scenarios while operating closely alongside tanks and artillery.

Their combination of mobility, protection, and firepower makes them a key component of modern military operations.

Special Capabilities

When in the same sector as an Armoured unit, Mechanised Infantry gains a +1 bonus to the Damage roll per unit level during combat.

Armoured Learn more about Armoured units and their role in combined-arms operations. Land Combat Learn how engagements between opposing ground forces are resolved.

Strengths

  • Strong balance of mobility and protection.
  • Effective in both offensive and defensive operations.
  • Excellent when operating alongside Armoured units.

Weaknesses

  • More expensive than Motorised Infantry.
  • Requires logistical support.

Recommended Employment

Use Mechanised Infantry as your main frontline combat unit for attacking and defending important sectors.
