Bomber

AIR

Overview

Bombers are long-range aircraft designed to deliver large quantities of ordnance against enemy ground targets and buildings. Their extended range and heavy payload allow them to strike targets deep within enemy territory and conduct strategic attacks against critical infrastructure.

Role

The primary role of the Bomber is to conduct long-range bombing operations against enemy ground targets and buildings. Bombers are specialised for strategic and ground attacks and cannot engage enemy air or naval units.

Combat Restrictions

Bombers can only attack ground units and buildings. They cannot attack enemy air or naval units.

Bombers are therefore dependent on other aircraft and naval forces to protect them from enemy air and naval threats.

Strengths

  • Extremely high offensive capability.
  • Very long operational range.
  • Effective against ground targets and buildings.
  • Capable of conducting attacks deep within enemy territory.

Weaknesses

  • Very limited defensive capability.
  • Cannot attack enemy air or naval units.
  • Expensive to build and maintain.
  • Vulnerable to enemy fighters and air defence.

Recommended Employment

Use Bombers to conduct strategic bombing operations against important enemy buildings and ground targets. Their long range allows them to strike targets beyond the frontline, but they should be protected from enemy aircraft and naval threats.
