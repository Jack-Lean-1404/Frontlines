AWACS

AWACS (Airborne Warning and Control System) units provide airborne command and control, enhancing the effectiveness of friendly air operations across a wide area.

AWACS Coverage

An AWACS unit supports friendly air units within a 2-sector radius.

Friendly air units within this coverage receive a 50% increase to Strength and Defence, representing improved situational awareness, coordination, and targeting.

AWACS effects do not stack. If multiple AWACS units cover the same friendly air unit, only one AWACS bonus is applied.

Combat Bonus

Friendly air units receive a 50% increase to Strength and Defence.

Non-Stacking

AWACS bonuses do not stack.

If a friendly air unit is within the coverage of multiple AWACS units, it receives the bonus from only one AWACS unit.

Protection of AWACS

AWACS units are high-value assets and are particularly vulnerable if left unprotected.

Protecting AWACS units is important because their destruction can significantly reduce the effectiveness of friendly air operations and coordination.

Strategic Role

AWACS provides a force multiplier for friendly air operations, allowing aircraft operating within its coverage to benefit from improved coordination and battlefield awareness.

AWACS is therefore most valuable when positioned where it can support multiple friendly aircraft while remaining protected from enemy attack.
